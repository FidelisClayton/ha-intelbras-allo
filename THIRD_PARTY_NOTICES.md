## quii-helper

The Go wire and media code adapts protocol methods and layouts from quii-helper: TCP setup and AES-CBC/SHA256 command envelopes, live PLAY header/body construction and reply validation, keepalive handling, CPacket framing and assembly, H264 frame extraction, native audio metadata, talk negotiation and packet layouts, and G.711 encoding. Relevant source modules include `protocols/tcp/live/session.py`, `protocols/tcp/live/packet_builder.py`, `protocols/quii/live_envelope.py`, `protocols/quii/live_bodies.py`, `media/cpacket/`, `media/frames/` and `media/audio/frames.py` and `protocols/tcp/talk/packets.py`. The independent encrypted PLAY fixture was generated by `QuiiLiveOpenPacketBuilder` with synthetic credentials.

Source used during development: https://github.com/xaer981/quii-helper. The upstream license follows verbatim.
